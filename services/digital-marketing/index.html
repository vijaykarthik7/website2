02 // MARKETING REALITIES

Ad budgets evaporate quickly. Why are your campaigns not generating profit?

Relying on vanity impressions and generic social posts burns capital without moving top-line revenue. Real digital growth requires technical attribution, conversion engineering, and high-intent targeting.

01 / RISING CAC

Skyrocketing Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC)

Ad auction competition and third-party cookie deprecation have inflated advertising costs. Running untargeted campaigns without algorithmic bidding and conversion rate optimization guarantees negative return on ad spend (ROAS).

02 / VANITY METRICS

The Illusion of Traffic Without Revenue

Generating millions of pageviews or social likes means nothing if visitors bounce within five seconds. Marketing must be judged by qualified sales inquiries, closed deals, and customer lifetime value (LTV).

03 / FRAGMENTED ATTRIBUTION

Opaque Attribution & Blind Spend

Without server-side conversion tracking and multi-touch attribution models, leadership cannot identify which channels actually drive revenue, leading to budget misallocation and wasted ad dollars.

CAPABILITY 05 // DATA ACCURACY

Server-Side Tracking & Multi-Touch Attribution

Browser-based tracking cookies are failing due to iOS privacy restrictions and ad blockers. We build enterprise server-side tracking pipelines using Google Tag Manager Server Container, Meta Conversions API (CAPI), and Cloudflare Workers. By sending first-party conversion data directly from your server to advertising platforms, we recover lost conversion signals, improve ad targeting algorithms, and give leadership transparent attribution metrics.

Problem Addressed
Lost conversion tracking from iOS users, ad platforms unable to optimize bidding, and inaccurate marketing reports.
Engineering Approach
Server-side GTM, Meta CAPI, Google Analytics 4 Measurement Protocol, and first-party cookie infrastructure.
Enterprise Use Case
E-commerce retailers, subscription services, high-value lead generation companies, and regulated enterprises.
Deliverables
Server-side tracking infrastructure, Meta CAPI deployment, custom attribution dashboard, and compliance audit.
