05 // TECHNICAL SPECIALIZATIONS

Engineering disciplines & architectural trade-offs

Every technical architecture requires balancing speed, operational complexity, and long-term maintainability. Here is how we evaluate core engineering disciplines for production stability.

DISCIPLINE 01

First-Party Server-Side Tagging

Conversion data is processed securely through server-side cloud workers, ensuring 100% data integrity without relying on vulnerable third-party client cookies.

Architectural Trade-Off Server-side tracking endpoints require cloud infrastructure configuration, but protect your attribution pipeline against ad-blocker loss.
DISCIPLINE 02

Message-Matched Dedicated Landing Pages

Every paid ad group connects directly to a tailored landing page reflecting the exact search keyword, maximizing Google Quality Scores and lowering cost-per-click.

Architectural Trade-Off Building dedicated landing pages per ad theme requires more development work, but cuts cost-per-lead by more than a third.
DISCIPLINE 03

Full-Funnel Unit Economics Focus

We measure success by cost-per-acquisition (CAC), customer lifetime value (LTV), and gross margin contributions rather than superficial vanity impressions.

Architectural Trade-Off Integrating CRM revenue data with ad platforms takes engineering coordination, but allows automatic algorithmic bidding on actual closed revenue.
