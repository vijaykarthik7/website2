02 // RETAIL REALITIES

Your store has traffic. Why are customers abandoning at checkout?

In e-commerce, the gap between a profitable brand and a struggling one often comes down to checkout speed, mobile UX friction, and inventory reliability under load.

01 / CHECKOUT FRICTION

Multi-Step Checkout Friction & Form Fatigue

Every additional form field, unexpected redirect, or delayed shipping calculator multiplies the likelihood of cart abandonment. Over 70% of digital shoppers abandon their orders due to cumbersome mobile checkout flows.

02 / FLASH TRAFFIC COLLAPSE

Peak Traffic Concurrency & Flash Sale Outages

Conventional monolithic store platforms crumble when marketing campaigns drive thousands of concurrent shoppers to a product drop. Database locks, inventory overselling, and checkout payment gateway timeouts destroy revenue.

03 / FRAGMENTED ERP SYNC

Inventory Desynchronization & Post-Purchase Friction

Selling an item that is out of stock in your warehouse triggers costly refunds, customer service escalations, and negative reviews. Modern commerce demands real-time bidirectional ERP/WMS inventory synchronization.
