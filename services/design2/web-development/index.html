Your website may look good. But is it doing its job?

The modern web is crowded with visually polished websites that fail at their primary commercial purpose. High bounce rates, drop-offs during key interactions, and unranked search positions are rarely cosmetic problems—they are structural engineering failures.

01 // REALITY CHECK

Bloated Client Bundles and Sluggish Load Times

Over-reliance on heavy client-side JavaScript frameworks pushes multi-megabyte payloads to mobile devices. On moderate cellular connections, users experience white screens, layout shifts, and unresponsive taps, causing high abandonment before the brand story even renders.

02 // REALITY CHECK

Unnavigable Information Architecture

When navigation mirrors internal corporate hierarchies rather than visitor mental models, prospective clients cannot find answers quickly. If pricing, service scope, or technical credibility remain buried under layers of marketing ambiguity, buyers exit.

03 // REALITY CHECK

Accessibility Barriers That Exclude and Expose

Websites built without strict semantic HTML, keyboard navigable states, and WCAG 2.2 AA compliance alienate millions of users with assistive technologies while exposing enterprise organizations to significant legal and regulatory liabilities.

04 // REALITY CHECK

Rigid Content Silos and Maintenance Paralysis

Legacy monolithic CMS setups trap marketing and engineering teams in conflicting release cycles. Simple copy updates require code deploys, while content creators struggle with fragile page builders that break layouts upon every update.
