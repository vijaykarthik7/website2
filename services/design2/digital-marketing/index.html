01 // REALITY CHECK

Zero-Click Search and AI Overviews

Over 60% of Google searches now end without a click to an external website as AI Overviews answer queries directly. Brands relying on basic informational keywords face steep traffic declines.

02 // REALITY CHECK

Skyrocketing Paid Ad Acquisition Costs

Customer acquisition costs (CAC) across Meta and Google Ads have climbed over 40% in two years. Blindly pumping budget into paid media without conversion rate optimization leads to negative margins.

03 // REALITY CHECK

Third-Party Cookie Deprecation and Broken Attribution

Browser tracking protections (ITP) and privacy regulations have broken traditional pixel tracking. Marketing leaders struggle to discern which channels truly generate enterprise deals.

04 // REALITY CHECK

Content Saturation and AI Slop Fatigue

The web is flooded with generic, automated AI blog posts that rank poorly and fail to convert. B2B buyers ignore superficial marketing fluff in favor of deep, authoritative technical analysis.
