02 // UX REALITIES

Your features are powerful. Why are users struggling to navigate them?

Feature bloat and confusing navigation kill software adoption. When software requires a user manual, users abandon it for simpler alternatives.

01 / COGNITIVE OVERLOAD

Visual Clutter & High Cognitive Load

Crowding screens with dozens of unorganized buttons, mismatched typography, and competing calls-to-action causes mental paralysis and user mistakes during high-stakes workflows.

02 / INCONSISTENT PATTERNS

Design Debt & Multi-Team Inconsistency

As development teams scale, buttons, modals, and input states diverge wildly across modules. Inconsistent UI patterns degrade brand credibility and confuse users.

03 / ACCESSIBILITY EXCLUSION

Low Contrast & Accessibility Violations

Over 95% of enterprise software interfaces fail basic accessibility audits. Inadequate color contrast and broken keyboard focus trap disabled users and invite legal exposure.

CAPABILITY 06 // COMPLIANCE & ETHICS

Accessibility Auditing & WCAG 2.2 AAA Compliance

Inaccessible digital products alienate millions of prospective customers and invite severe legal penalties under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and the European Accessibility Act (EAA). We perform rigorous accessibility audits of existing and new digital products, testing screen reader navigation (VoiceOver, NVDA), keyboard-only tab sequences, dynamic text resizing, and color contrast compliance to guarantee certified accessibility.

Problem Addressed
Legal liability from accessibility lawsuits, screen readers unable to read unlabeled buttons, and invisible focus rings.
Engineering Approach
Manual screen reader testing, automated axe-core scanning, semantic ARIA token mapping, and color contrast verification.
Enterprise Use Case
Public sector digital services, banking and insurance portals, healthcare systems, and educational platforms.
Deliverables
Comprehensive WCAG 2.2 audit report, remediation design specs, accessibility statement, and compliance certificate.
