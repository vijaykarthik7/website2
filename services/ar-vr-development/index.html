SECTION 02 // STRATEGIC REALITY

Some experiences are easier to understand when you can step inside them.

Spatial computing is far more than entertainment headsets. When enterprise training simulations allow industrial technicians or surgeons to rehearse high-risk procedures without physical danger, spatial technology translates directly into saved lives and millions in prevented downtime.

Review Our Solutions →
01 // REALITY CHECK

Simulation Sickness and Motion-to-Photon Lag

When frame rates dip below 90 FPS or tracking latency exceeds 20 milliseconds, the human vestibular system experiences severe motion sickness, rendering the experience unusable.

02 // REALITY CHECK

Fatiguing Spatial Ergonomics and Heavy Hand Gestures

Forcing users to hold their arms outstretched in mid-air (the "gorilla arm" problem) creates rapid physical fatigue. Spatial interfaces must be designed for relaxed micro-gestures.

03 // REALITY CHECK

Hardware Fragmentation and Varied Field-of-View

Building for the Apple Vision Pro, Meta Quest 3, HoloLens 2, and WebXR on smartphones requires careful abstraction of optical pass-through versus immersive VR optics.

04 // REALITY CHECK

Complex CAD Asset Bloat and Low-Poly Retopology

Directly importing raw engineering CAD files with tens of millions of micro-polygons causes immediate headset crashes. Assets must undergo disciplined automated retopology.
