05 // TECHNICAL SPECIALIZATIONS

Engineering disciplines & architectural trade-offs

Every technical architecture requires balancing speed, operational complexity, and long-term maintainability. Here is how we evaluate core engineering disciplines for production stability.

DISCIPLINE 01

Accurate Environmental Physics & Collision

Virtual objects respond realistically to gravity, inertia, and physical surface collisions, delivering believable interactions.

Architectural Trade-Off Real-time raycasting against room meshes adds CPU calculations, but prevents digital objects from unnaturally floating in space.
DISCIPLINE 02

Spatial Audio Acoustics Simulation

Sound waves reflect, occlude, and attenuate based on physical room geometry, creating convincing three-dimensional acoustic presence.

Architectural Trade-Off Binaural audio filtering requires dedicated audio threads, but anchors player spatial orientation in virtual spaces.
DISCIPLINE 03

Foveated Rendering Optimization

We concentrate GPU rendering power directly where the user’s gaze is focused, preserving visual clarity while conserving processing budget.

Architectural Trade-Off Eye-tracking integration is hardware-dependent, but yields up to 40% higher graphical fidelity at identical frame rates.
